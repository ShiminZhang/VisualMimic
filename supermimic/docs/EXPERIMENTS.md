# Historical experiments

The archived LAFAN1 experiments used 40 clips. The single-GPU e2 run with paper observations reported a tracking score of 0.56 after 1.8 hours. The e3 run added anchor-relative observations and reported 0.824 after 1.66 hours and 0.904 after 3.3 hours. Observation settings differ across these runs. These historical training results are independent of the CPU smoke test.
